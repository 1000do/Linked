1.2 Project Objectives

The overall goal of the Online Course Marketplace project is to develop and implement a comprehensive online course platform that meets the diverse needs of learning enthusiasts, including both beginners and advanced users. This system aims to simplify the learning and research process, improve user capacity and engagement, and ensure a safe and responsive learning environment.

The platform will integrate key functionalities such as personalized learning plans, detailed course guides, instructor interaction, and community engagement to promote long-term learning goals. It also includes study management tools and supports scalability.

Timeliness (%):

  • Ensure the entire system development and deployment are completed within the planned timeline.
  • Target: 95% of project tasks must be completed on time, as per the project schedule.

Allocated Effort (man-days):

  • Optimize the use of development and design resources to deliver all core functionalities within the planned effort.
  • Target: Maintain total man-day utilization within the initial estimated project effort budget.

Defect Distribution:

  • Target:

2. Management Approach

Figure 1. Management Approach Diagram

Scrum, a flexible development framework, serves as the foundation for the Online Course Marketplace's development process. Its iterative and incremental approach ensures adaptability, flexibility, and efficiency, allowing the team to continuously deliver value to users. In the fast-paced world of software development, where diverse roles must collaborate seamlessly, Scrum acts as a guiding methodology, ensuring that developers, designers, AI engineers, and testers work in sync to build a high-quality, user-centric fitness platform.

At its core, Scrum focuses on meeting user needs through transparent communication, collective responsibility, and continuous improvement. The development journey begins with a high-level product vision, refined into a prioritized backlog aligned with goals defined by the Product Owner. Each feature undergoes iterative development through structured Sprints, ensuring that each release delivers meaningful improvements to the platform.

By adopting Scrum, the Online Course Marketplace fosters a collaborative, feedback-driven, and innovation-focused development environment, enabling the creation of an AI-powered fitness platform that evolves with user needs and industry trends.

2.1 Project Process

The Online Course Marketplace project follows the Scrum framework, dividing the development process into iterative cycles (sprints) typically lasting 3 to 4 months. Each sprint provides a specific set of functionalities or incremental improvements, ensuring continuous progress and alignment with user needs. Tasks within each sprint are carefully categorized, developed, and evaluated to meet initial goals within a defined timeframe.

During project execution, the team conducts several essential meetings within each sprint, including:

  • Sprint Planning Meeting: Defines tasks and goals for the upcoming sprint.
  • Daily Briefing Meeting: A brief meeting to discuss progress, challenges, and next steps.
  • Sprint Review Meeting: Evaluates completed work and updates the Product Backlog if necessary.
  • Sprint Retrospective Meeting: Identify areas for improvement before starting the next sprint.

Additionally, the project progresses through consecutive sprints, with continuous refinement of the Product Backlog to align with updated requirements. Each day, the team holds a daily meeting to review the functionalities completed the previous day and address any obstacles. At the end of each sprint, a Sprint Review Meeting is held, lasting approximately 3-4 hours, where the team and stakeholders assess progress and discuss potential adjustments to the Product Backlog. Following this, a Sprint Retrospective Meeting (lasting 1.5 hours) is conducted to review the sprint's effectiveness and identify areas for ongoing improvement.

Throughout this process, it is crucial to maintain stability in the sprint objectives, ensuring that no changes negatively impact the sprint's goals. The functions and requirements must meet quality standards and be clearly defined through collaboration between the Product Owner and the Development Team. By adhering to this structured Scrum process, the Online Course Marketplace ensures an efficient, iterative, and high-quality development cycle.

2.2 Quality Management

To improve project quality and achieve quality goals, a holistic approach is necessary. Below are the key quality approaches, each contributing to the overall improvement of the Online Course Marketplace platform:

Error Prevention

  • Cultivate a proactive mindset within the team to identify and resolve potential issues before they become critical.
  • Conduct thorough requirements analysis to avoid misunderstandings and ambiguity.
  • Apply coding standards and best practices to minimize errors during development.

Review

  • Regularly review code to ensure compliance with coding standards and identify potential issues early.
  • Implement a peer review process for documentation, requirements, and design products to improve accuracy.
  • Foster a collaborative environment for constructive feedback to enhance overall quality.

Test-Driven Development in Unit Testing

  • Applying Test-Driven Development (TDD) promotes a test-first approach, ensuring code meets predefined requirements.
  • Writing unit tests before code deployment to detect errors early and minimize debugging efforts later.
  • Refactoring code to improve structure and maintainability while ensuring test cases remain valid.

Integration Testing

  • Performing integration testing to verify the correct operation of combined system components.
  • Developing a robust test environment that closely simulates production conditions.
  • Implementing Continuous Integration (CI) to automate testing and detect integration issues early.

System Testing

  • Conducting comprehensive system testing to validate overall functionality and performance.
  • Create test scenarios encompassing various use cases, including training progress tracking, AI analysis, and payment processing.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to define acceptance criteria and meet user expectations.

Continuous Monitoring and Feedback

  • Implement real-time monitoring to detect and resolve issues promptly.
  • Gather user feedback through surveys and in-app reports to ensure alignment with user expectations.
  • Utilize feedback loops to continuously improve the platform throughout the development process.

Scrum Process Integration

  • Incorporate the Scrum methodology to enhance flexibility and adaptability.
  • Regular sprint planning ensures task prioritization and efficient resource allocation.
  • Daily briefing meetings foster communication and transparency among team members.
  • Sprint reviews provide an opportunity for stakeholders to assess progress and provide feedback.
  • Post-sprint meetings encourage continuous improvement and workflow optimization.

Quality Metrics and Reporting

  • Identify key quality metrics (e.g., error rate, response time, AI accuracy) to measure progress.
  • Regularly monitor and report metrics to identify areas for improvement.
  • Utilize data-driven insights to implement corrective actions and maintain quality.

Training and Skill Development

  • Provide training sessions on software testing, AI model validation, and security best practices.
  • Encourage a culture of continuous learning to help the team stay up-to-date with industry standards.
