3.11.2 View Balance

The purpose of this workflow is to allow the Instructor to view their current available balance, pending funds, and overall financial summaries.

Execution steps:

Step 1: The Instructor accesses the Dashboard and clicks the Revenue Dashboard menu item to open the Revenue & Payouts dashboard page.

Step 2: The system displays the financial summary cards including Gross Revenue, Total Refunded, Net Earnings, Pending transfer, Stripe Wallet, Withdrawn amounts, and the Transaction Revenue Chart.
