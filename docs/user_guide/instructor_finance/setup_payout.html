3.11.1 Setup Payout

The purpose of this workflow is to allow the Instructor to configure Stripe Connect bank/account details to receive platform payouts.

Execution steps:

Step 1: The Instructor accesses the Dashboard and clicks the Application Status menu item to open the Instructor Application Status page.

Step 2: When the application is approved, the Instructor clicks the Connect Stripe — Unlock Premium Pricing button.

(Insert a screenshot of the Instructor Application Status page here, highlighting the Connect Stripe button in red)

Figure 1. Setup Payout operation

Step 3: The system redirects the Instructor to the Stripe Connect onboarding page. The Instructor inputs their registration information (e.g., Email address, Phone number, Bank account details) and clicks the Submit button to complete the setup process.

(Insert a screenshot of the Stripe Connect onboarding page here, highlighting the input fields and Submit button in red)

Figure 2. Stripe Connect onboarding operation

Step 4: The system prompts the Instructor to verify their phone number. The Instructor inputs the 6-digit verification code sent via text message to successfully continue.

(Insert a screenshot of the Stripe verification code page here, highlighting the code input fields in red)

Figure 3. Stripe phone verification operation

Step 5: The system prompts the Instructor to provide business details. The Instructor selects the Business type (e.g., Individual) and clicks the Continue button.

(Insert a screenshot of the Stripe Business type selection page here, highlighting the Business type dropdown and Continue button in red)

Figure 4. Stripe business details operation

Step 6: The system prompts the Instructor to verify their personal details. The Instructor inputs information / selects actions:

  • Your legal name: First and last name as it appears in government records.
  • Email address: Instructor's contact email.
  • Date of birth: Instructor's date of birth.
  • Home address: Instructor's residential address.
  • Phone number: A valid phone number in the country of the account.
  • Last 4 digits of Social Security number: Required to satisfy regulatory obligations.

Step 7: The Instructor clicks the Continue button.

(Insert a screenshot of the Stripe Verify your personal details page here, highlighting the input fields and Continue button in red)

Figure 5. Stripe personal details operation

Step 8: The system prompts the Instructor to provide further business details. The Instructor inputs information / selects actions:

  • Industry: A dropdown to select the industry that best matches the provided goods or services (e.g., Software).
  • Product description: A text area to enter a brief description of the products or services sold.

Step 9: The Instructor clicks the Continue button.

(Insert a screenshot of the Stripe Business details page here, highlighting the input fields and Continue button in red)

Figure 6. Stripe business details form operation

Step 10: The system prompts the Instructor to select an account for payouts. The Instructor inputs information / selects actions:

  • Bank institution: Search and select a bank institution from the list, or choose to enter bank account credentials manually.

Step 11: The Instructor clicks the Continue button.

(Insert a screenshot of the Stripe Bank selection page here, highlighting the institution selection and Continue button in red)

Figure 7. Stripe bank account selection operation

Step 12: The system displays the Review and submit page summarizing the provided information.

Step 13: If any section is marked as "Incomplete" or "Invalid" (e.g., Personal details), the Instructor clicks the Edit button for that section.

Step 14: The system prompts the Instructor to provide Additional information (e.g., SSN/ITIN). The Instructor inputs the required data and clicks Submit.

Step 15: The system prompts the Instructor to Verify your identity. The Instructor clicks Start verification and follows the instructions to upload a government-issued photo ID (e.g., scanning a QR code on a mobile device).

Step 16: Once verification is successfully processed, the system returns the Instructor to the Review and submit page, where the updated section is now marked as "Verified" with a green badge.

Step 17: The Instructor clicks the Agree and submit button to finish the Stripe payout setup and return to the platform.
