3.11.4 View Payout History

The purpose of this workflow is to allow the Instructor to view a history of their payout withdrawal records.

Execution steps:

Step 1: The Instructor accesses the Dashboard and clicks the Revenue Dashboard menu item to open the Revenue & Payouts dashboard page.

Step 2: The Instructor clicks the Payout History tab.

Step 3: The system displays a table of past payouts including Date, Status, and Amount.
