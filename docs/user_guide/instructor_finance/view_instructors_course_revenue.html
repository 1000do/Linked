3.11.9 View Instructor's Course Revenue

The purpose of this workflow is to allow the Instructor to display course-wise revenue analysis and earnings reports.

Execution steps:

Step 1: The Instructor accesses the Dashboard and clicks the Revenue Dashboard menu item to open the Revenue & Payouts dashboard page.

Step 2: The Instructor clicks the Course Revenues tab.

Step 3: The system displays a table of revenue analysis broken down by each course.
