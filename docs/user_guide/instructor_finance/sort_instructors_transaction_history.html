3.11.5 Sort Instructor's Transaction History

The purpose of this workflow is to allow the Instructor to sort course sales transaction records by date or amount.

Execution steps:

Step 1: The Instructor accesses the Learner Purchases tab on the Revenue & Payouts dashboard page.

Step 2: The Instructor inputs information / selects actions:

  • Sort By: A dropdown menu to select the desired sorting criteria (Date: Newest, Date: Oldest, Amount: High to Low, or Amount: Low to High).

Step 3: The Instructor clicks the Filter button.
