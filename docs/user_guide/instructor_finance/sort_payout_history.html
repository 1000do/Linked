3.11.8 Sort Payout History

The purpose of this workflow is to allow the Instructor to sort payout withdrawal records by date or amount.

Execution steps:

Step 1: The Instructor accesses the Dashboard and clicks the Revenue Dashboard menu item to open the Revenue & Payouts dashboard page.

Step 2: The Instructor clicks the Payout History tab.

Step 3: The Instructor inputs information / selects actions:

  • Sort By: A dropdown menu to select the sorting criteria (Date: Newest, Date: Oldest, Amount: High to Low, or Amount: Low to High).

Step 4: The Instructor clicks the Filter button.
