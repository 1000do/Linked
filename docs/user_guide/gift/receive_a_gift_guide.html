3.19.3 Receive a gift

The purpose of this workflow is to allow the recipient to view the gift card and redeem the gifted course.

Execution steps:

Step 1: The recipient accesses the Gift Claim page by clicking the gift notification link from the in-app notification dropdown menu.

(Insert a screenshot of the in-app notification panel here, highlighting the gifted course notification link in red)

Figure 1. Access from in-app notification

Step 2: Alternatively, the recipient accesses the Gift Claim page by opening the gift notification email in Gmail and clicking the redemption link.

(Insert a screenshot of the gift notification email in Gmail here, highlighting the redemption link or button in red)

Figure 2. Access from Gmail notification

Step 3: The recipient reviews the customized gift card and course information:

  • Sender Name: Read-only. Displays the name of the sender who sent the course gift card.
  • Recipient Greeting: Read-only. Displays a personalized greeting addressed to the recipient.
  • Gift Message: Read-only. Displays the custom personal message written by the sender, or a default greeting if no custom message was provided.
  • Gift Course: Read-only. Displays the course thumbnail and title of the gifted online course.

(Insert a screenshot of the Gift Claim page here, highlighting the gift card details and Claim Your Course Now button in red)

Figure 3. Receive a gift operation

Step 4: The recipient clicks the Claim Your Course Now button to redeem the gifted course.
