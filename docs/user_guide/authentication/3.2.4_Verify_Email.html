3.2.4 Verify Email

The purpose of this workflow is to allow the Account Owner to verify their email address using a One-Time Password (OTP).

Execution steps:

Step 1: The Account Owner accesses the Verify OTP page.

Step 2: The Account Owner inputs information:

  • Enter OTP Code: The 6-digit OTP code received in the email. This field is required.
